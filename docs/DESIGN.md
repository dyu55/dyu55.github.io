# Michael Yu — Frost Blue palette

A personal palette centered on frost-blue surfaces, ink typography and a restrained wine-red signature.

| Role | Light theme | Dark theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#F6F9FC` cold white | `#101D2A` deep ink |
| Main text | `#172D40` ink blue | `#EDF4FA` ice white |
| Frost panel | `#C5DCED` frost blue | `#29485F` blue slate |
| Panel text | `#172D40` ink blue | `#EDF4FA` ice white |
| Secondary panel text | `#435B70` steel | `#C7DCEB` pale frost |
| Links and focus | `#305F82` deep blue | `#B4D4EC` pale blue |
| Signature accent | `#783E4E` wine red | `#E3AFBD` muted rose |

Frost blue carries the introduction panel, contact section and name underline. Ink anchors typography and primary buttons. Wine red is reserved for the initials and small section labels; it is not a large surface. Project screenshots retain their actual application colors.

Colors are centralized in `src/app/globals.css`. Browser theme metadata follows the background colors. The sixteen checked body, panel, link, signature and button text/background combinations have calculated contrast ratios of approximately 5:1 or higher. This is a palette calculation, not a full browser accessibility audit.
